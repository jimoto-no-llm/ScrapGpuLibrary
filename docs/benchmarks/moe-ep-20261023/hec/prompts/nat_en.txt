The old lighthouse keeper had lived on the rock for thirty-one winters, and in all that time he had never seen the sea behave the way it did on the night the supply boat failed to arrive. The barometer fell through the afternoon, the gulls went inland, and by dusk the swell was breaking over the lower steps of the landing stage. He lit the lamp early, checked the oil twice, and then sat down at the table with the logbook open in front of him, because he knew that whatever happened next would have to be written down.
